Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the most common questions about Diecast.scape, our products, custom orders, payments, shipping, and more.

About Diecast.scape

What is Diecast.scape?

Diecast.scape is a small independent brand focused on creating handcrafted products for diecast collectors, miniature enthusiasts, photographers, and automotive lovers.

What products do you offer?

We offer 1:64 scale dioramas, miniature accessories, and other handcrafted products designed to enhance and display your collection.

Are your products handmade?

Yes. Our products are carefully handcrafted and produced in limited quantities rather than being mass-produced.

What scale are your products made for?

Most of our products are designed around the 1:64 scale used by many diecast collectors. The scale and compatibility of each product are mentioned in its respective product details.

Dioramas

Are the diecast cars included with the diorama?

Unless specifically mentioned in the product listing, diecast cars are not included with the diorama. The cars shown in product images may be used for display and presentation purposes.

Can I request a custom diorama?

Yes. Custom designs may be possible depending on the requested design, materials, dimensions, and complexity. You can contact us with your requirements or reference images for discussion.

Can I change the design of an existing diorama?

Certain elements such as colours, layout, lighting, accessories, or other design details may be customizable depending on the product. Customization will be confirmed before production.

Do the dioramas come assembled?

Our dioramas are prepared and assembled according to the product design. Any specific assembly or installation requirements will be communicated with the order where applicable.

Do your dioramas have lighting?

Some diorama designs include lighting. The availability of lighting, adapter requirements, and other electrical details are mentioned in the individual product listing.

How many cars can a diorama hold?

Capacity depends on the design and dimensions of the particular diorama. The recommended capacity is mentioned in the product specifications where applicable.

Miniature Accessories

What are the miniature accessories used for?

Our miniature accessories are designed to add realistic details to dioramas, displays, photography setups, and diecast collections.

What scale are the accessories?

Our accessories are primarily designed for 1:64 scale displays. Compatibility may vary depending on the type and design of the accessory.

How many pieces are included?

The quantity varies by product. The exact quantity or set size is shown on the respective product listing.

Can I order multiple sets?

Yes. You can order multiple quantities of available accessories, subject to stock availability.

Can I request a custom miniature accessory?

Custom accessory requests may be possible depending on the design and production requirements. Contact us with your reference or requirements to discuss availability.

Orders & Payments

How can I place an order?

Available products can be ordered through the website. For custom requirements or products that require discussion, you can contact us directly through WhatsApp or Instagram.

Is an advance payment required?

For custom handcrafted orders, a 50% advance payment is required to confirm the order and begin production.

When do I pay the remaining amount?

The remaining 50% payment is required after the product has been completed and before the order is dispatched.

Why is advance payment required?

Our products are handcrafted and often prepared specifically for the customer. The advance payment allows us to confirm the order and begin purchasing materials and production.

Can I change my order after placing it?

Changes may be possible before production begins. Once production or customization has started, changes may not be possible or may result in additional charges depending on the request.

Custom Orders

Can I provide a reference image for a custom product?

Yes. You can share reference images, sketches, dimensions, colour preferences, or other requirements with us before the order is confirmed.

How is the price of a custom product decided?

Custom pricing depends on factors such as size, materials, design complexity, number of components, lighting, accessories, and production requirements. The final price will be discussed before confirmation.

How long does a custom order take?

Custom products generally require approximately 2–3 days to build after the required advance payment and order details have been confirmed. More complex projects may require additional time.

Shipping & Delivery

Do you ship across India?

Yes. We currently provide shipping within India.

Which shipping service do you use?

Orders are generally shipped through India Post with suitable and safe packaging.

Are shipping charges applicable?

Shipping charges may apply depending on the product and order. Any applicable shipping charges will be shown or communicated before the order is finalized.

When will my order be shipped?

Custom orders are dispatched after production is completed and the remaining payment has been received.

Will I receive tracking information?

Where tracking is available, the tracking details may be shared with the customer after dispatch.

What if my delivery is delayed?

Delivery times may vary depending on the destination, postal service, weather, holidays, local conditions, and other circumstances outside our control.

For complete information, please see our Shipping Policy.

Returns & Cancellation

Can I return a product?

As our products are handcrafted and made in limited quantities, returns are generally not accepted once an order has been confirmed.

Can I cancel my order?

Cancellations may be accepted before the product is shipped. Once the product has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible.

What if my product arrives damaged?

If your order arrives damaged, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with clear photographs and a complete unboxing video recorded from the beginning of opening the package without any cuts.

Why is an unboxing video required?

A complete unboxing video helps us verify the condition of the package and product at the time of delivery and allows us to properly review damage-related claims.

For complete details, please read our Return & Cancellation Policy.

Care & Handling

How should I clean my diorama?

Use a soft, dry microfiber cloth or a gentle brush to remove dust. Avoid applying excessive pressure to delicate miniature elements.

How should I clean acrylic surfaces?

Clean acrylic surfaces gently using a soft microfiber cloth. Avoid abrasive materials or harsh chemicals that may scratch or damage the surface.

How should I handle miniature accessories?

Miniature accessories contain small and delicate components. Handle them carefully and keep them away from children and pets where appropriate.

Can I keep my diorama in direct sunlight?

We recommend keeping handcrafted displays away from prolonged direct sunlight, excessive heat, moisture, and other conditions that may affect materials, colours, acrylic, or miniature components over time.

Contact Us

How can I contact Diecast.scape?

For questions about products, custom orders, payments, or existing orders, you can contact us through WhatsApp or Instagram.

When contacting us about an existing order, please provide the relevant order details so that we can assist you more efficiently.
