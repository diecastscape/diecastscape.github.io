Shipping Policy

At Diecast.scape, our products are custom handcrafted and carefully prepared for each order. Because of this, orders are processed and shipped only after the required production and payment steps are completed.

Order Processing

A 50% advance payment is required to confirm and begin work on custom handcrafted orders.

Once the product has been completed, the remaining 50% payment must be received before the order is dispatched.

For products that are ready to ship, the payment and dispatch process may be completed sooner depending on availability.

Production Time

Custom products generally require approximately 2–3 days to build after the required advance payment and order details have been confirmed.

Production time may vary depending on the product, customization requirements, order volume, material availability, or other circumstances. Any significant delay will be communicated to the customer.

Shipping Method

Orders within India are generally shipped through India Post using safe and suitable packaging for the product.

Shipping charges are applicable unless a specific offer or product listing states otherwise.

Packaging

Each order is packed carefully to reduce the risk of damage during transportation. As our products may contain delicate handcrafted and miniature components, customers are advised to handle the package carefully after delivery.

Delivery & Tracking

Once the order has been dispatched, shipping or tracking details may be shared with the customer where available.

Delivery time depends on the courier or postal service, destination, local delivery conditions, holidays, weather, and other factors outside our direct control.

Diecast.scape is not responsible for delays caused by the shipping carrier or circumstances beyond our control.

Delivery Address

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address and contact number when placing an order.

Any additional shipping costs resulting from an incorrect, incomplete, or changed delivery address may be charged to the customer.

Damaged Packages

If an order appears damaged upon delivery, please record a complete unboxing video from the beginning of opening the package without any cuts or interruptions.

For damage-related assistance, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with clear photographs and the required unboxing video.

For more information regarding damaged products, returns, and cancellations, please refer to our Return & Cancellation Policy.

Shipping Questions

If you have any questions regarding shipping, delivery, or your order, you can contact us through WhatsApp or Instagram. We will be happy to assist you.
