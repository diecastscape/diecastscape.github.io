Privacy Policy

At Diecast.scape, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how we handle it.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when placing an order, requesting customization, contacting us, or communicating with us through available contact channels.

This information may include your name, phone number, delivery address, order details, customization requirements, and other information necessary to communicate with you and process your request.

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used only for legitimate business purposes, including processing and fulfilling orders, communicating with customers, discussing customization requests, providing order-related updates, and providing customer support.

We may also use information you provide to clarify product requirements, confirm order details, or respond to questions and requests.

WhatsApp & Other Communication

If you contact Diecast.scape through WhatsApp, Instagram, or another communication platform, the information you provide through that platform may be visible to us and may be used to respond to your enquiry or process your order.

Your use of those platforms is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Payment Information

Diecast.scape does not require customers to share unnecessary payment information through our website or WhatsApp conversations.

Where payment is arranged through a third-party payment service, bank, UPI service, or other payment provider, payment information may be handled by that respective service according to its own policies.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information for marketing purposes.

Information may only be shared when reasonably necessary to fulfill an order, provide a requested service, comply with applicable legal requirements, or protect the rights and security of Diecast.scape and its customers.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information provided to us from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic communication or internet-based storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Website Information

Our website may automatically collect limited technical information such as browser type, device information, general usage information, and website interaction data through standard web technologies or analytics services.

This information may be used to understand website usage, improve website performance, and provide a better browsing experience.

Google Analytics

Diecast.scape uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the website. This may involve the collection of general usage and technical information through cookies or similar technologies.

Analytics information is used to understand website traffic and improve the website. It is not used by Diecast.scape to intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for website functionality, analytics, or improving the user experience.

You can manage or restrict cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services or platforms for functions such as analytics, communication, hosting, payment processing, or other website-related services.

Information handled by those services may be subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Data Retention

We retain customer information only for as long as reasonably necessary for order processing, customer support, business records, legal requirements, or other legitimate business purposes.

Your Privacy

You may contact us if you have questions about personal information you have provided to Diecast.scape or if you would like clarification about how your information is being used.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, business practices, or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, you can contact Diecast.scape through our official WhatsApp or Instagram contact channels.
