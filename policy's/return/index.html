Return & Cancellation Policy

At Diecast.scape, our products are handcrafted and produced in limited quantities. Many of our products are prepared specifically after an order is confirmed. Because of this, we follow the return and cancellation conditions below.

Order Cancellation

Order cancellation requests can be made before the product has been shipped. Once an order has entered the final production stage or has already been shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible.

If you wish to cancel an order, please contact us as soon as possible through WhatsApp or Instagram with your order details.

Advance Payment & Cancellation

For custom and handcrafted orders, an advance payment may be required before production begins. Once production has started, the advance may not be refundable because materials, time, and production work may already have been committed to the order.

Any cancellation or refund request will be reviewed based on the stage of production and the circumstances of the order.

Returns

As our products are handcrafted and made in limited quantities, we generally do not accept returns for change of mind, personal preference, incorrect selection, or other reasons unrelated to a product defect or delivery issue.

Please carefully review the product details, dimensions, images, specifications, and other available information before placing your order. If you have any questions about a product, you can contact us before ordering.

Damaged Products

We take care to package products securely before dispatch. However, damage can occasionally occur during transportation.

If your package arrives damaged, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery and provide clear photographs of the package, the product, and the damaged area.

A complete unboxing video recorded continuously from the beginning of opening the package until the product has been fully inspected, without cuts or edits, is mandatory for damage-related claims.

The unboxing video and photographs may be required to verify the condition of the package and product at the time of delivery.

Wrong or Missing Items

If you receive an incorrect product, an incorrect quantity, or an item is missing from your order, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with photographs and a complete unboxing video.

After reviewing the provided information, we will discuss the appropriate resolution with you.

Handcrafted Product Variations

Our products are handcrafted and may have minor variations in finishing, texture, colour, placement, or other small details. Such variations are a natural part of handmade production and may not be considered defects.

Product images are provided to represent the design and appearance of the product. Minor differences may occur between the displayed images and the finished handcrafted product.

Damage After Delivery

Damage caused after delivery due to improper handling, accidental damage, modifications, installation, misuse, or normal wear and tear is not covered under the damage-related assistance described above.

Refunds & Replacements

Refunds or replacements are not automatic. If a product is found to have arrived damaged, incorrect, or incomplete, the case will be reviewed based on the evidence provided.

Where applicable, a refund, replacement, repair, or another suitable resolution may be discussed after the review.

Each case is reviewed individually to ensure a fair and reasonable resolution based on the circumstances of the order.

How to Contact Us

For cancellation requests, damaged products, incorrect items, or other order-related concerns, please contact Diecast.scape through our official WhatsApp or Instagram contact channels as soon as possible.

Please keep your order details, photographs, videos, and other relevant information available when contacting us so that we can review your request properly.
